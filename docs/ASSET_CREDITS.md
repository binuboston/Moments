# Asset credits

Sample photographs in `public/samples/` are used **only** for template previews, the catalogue and live demos. They are labelled as sample photography in the product and are never presented as photos of a real couple. Real invitations use photos uploaded by their owners.

All images come from Wikimedia Commons. They were resized and re-encoded to WebP (a modification under CC BY / CC BY-SA terms); modified copies are shared under the same licence as the original.
